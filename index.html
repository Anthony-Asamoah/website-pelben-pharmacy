About Pelben Pharmacy


We are a reliable pharmacy committed to delivering quality service for all types of health needs.

Our team of skilled pharmacists provides a full range of services including prescription fulfillment, health consultations, and medication management. We use modern tools to quickly identify and address your health concerns, saving you time and money. Grounded in honesty, efficiency, and genuine care, we ensure every person who walks through our doors receives the dedicated attention they deserve.

Our Values


Mission: To elevate community wellness by providing honest, efficient, and affordable pharmacy services. We are dedicated to saving our patients time and money while ensuring every individual receives the personalized care and attention they deserve.


Vision: To be the leading community pharmacy recognized for transforming the healthcare experience — where accessing honest, efficient, and affordable care is effortless, empowering every family in our community to live healthier, happier lives.
